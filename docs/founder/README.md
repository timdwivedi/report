# Your App Brain Dump

> **Drop everything about your app here.** The more you share, the better the AI understands your vision.

## What to Put Here

Literally anything related to your app idea:

- **Notes & docs** -- Google Docs, Notion exports, text files, whatever you have
- **Conversations** -- ChatGPT logs, voice note transcripts, messages with your team
- **Competitor research** -- Screenshots, links, notes about apps you like or want to be better than
- **Design inspiration** -- Screenshots of apps/websites whose look you love
- **Your audience** -- Who they are, what they struggle with, what they've told you
- **Feature ideas** -- Every feature you've ever thought of, even rough ones
- **Business model** -- Pricing ideas, how you plan to make money
- **Brand assets** -- Logo files, color codes, fonts you like

## Format Doesn't Matter

- `.md`, `.txt`, `.pdf` -- all good
- Name files however you want
- Messy is fine. The AI will organize it.

## What Happens Next

When you say "setup project" or "get started", the AI will:

1. Read everything in this folder
2. Understand your vision, audience, and goals
3. Ask you smart follow-up questions (only what's missing)
4. Create a detailed project spec that guides the entire build

**The better your brain dump, the less questions you'll be asked and the more accurate the first build will be.**
