## Company Overview

**85 Supply** is a full-service merch agency based in Nashville, TN. They are NOT a local t-shirt printer — they position as a concierge-level merch agency. They don't manufacture in-house; they subcontract through trusted printers who drop-ship blind in 85 Supply branded packaging.

1. **Supply Side (Bread & Butter):** Transactional B2B orders. Company has event → needs 100 t-shirts, hats, hoodies → 85 Supply handles everything. Average deal: $5K–$12K. Very transactional, closes within days.

2. **E-Commerce:** Creating and managing web stores for entertainment artists, corporations, schools, churches. Revenue-generating but not the primary focus.
