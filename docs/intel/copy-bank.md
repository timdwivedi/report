## Core Industry Terminology

### Product Types
| Term | Definition | Trevor's Words |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Contract Product** | A blank garment purchased separately and sent to a decorator for printing/embroidery. Pricing = blank cost + decoration cost + margin. | "A contract product has a blank, has a blank cost... you're buying the blank apparel, you're giving it to a local screen print shop... and they are going to decorate that product." |
| **All-In Product** | A product ordered complete from a single vendor (e.g., coffee mug, koozie). One vendor, one cost. | "An all-in product is going to be something more along the lines of a coffee mug where we're going to send that purchase order to one single supplier." |
| **Blank** / **Blank Cost** | The raw, undecorated garment and its wholesale cost. | "A blank, blank, it has a blank cost." |
| **Small through XL** | Standard size range where blank cost is usually uniform. | "Your small through XL is always usually going to be the same. And then the 2X is going to be different, 3 and 4X is going to be different." |

### Decoration & Printing
| Term | Definition | Trevor's Words |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Decoration Location** | A specific print/embroidery position on a garment (front, back, sleeve, left chest, neckline). Each is a standalone object. | "You add a decoration location, add, maybe that's front, add one, that's back." |
| **Decoration Method** | The type of printing/embroidery (screen print, embroidery, DTG, heat transfer, digital squeegee). | "What charge is it? Screen printing, digital squeegee, embroidery." |
| **Screen Printing** | Traditional press-based printing. Priced by **print colors** (number of ink colors used). | "Contract screen printing is based on print colors." |
| **Embroidery** | Thread-based decoration. Priced by **stitch count**. | "Embroidery pricing is based on stitch count. Stitch count is the amount of stitches it will take to successfully embroider that logo." |
| **DTG (Direct-to-Garment)** | Digital printing method for low minimums and full-color designs. | "It's a digital, it's meant for low minimum order quantities. It's called direct-to-garment printing." |
| **Heat Transfer** | Decoration method using heat-applied graphics. | Referenced in matrices and product settings. |
| **Puff Ink** | Specialty ink that creates a raised/3D texture on garments. | "If our white star is for a streetwear client, and they want that white star puffed... maybe that run charge for puff inks might be a dollar a unit at 24, 75 cents at 36." |
| **Printed Tags** | Custom neck labels printed inside the neckline, replacing manufacturer tags. | "Printed tags, have you guys ever seen printed tags where they pull the manufacturer's tag of the neckline, and then they print your own private tag right there." |
| **Specialty Inks** | Metallic inks, glow in the dark, etc. Priced as run charges. | "Specialty inks, metallic inks, glow in the dark, et cetera." |
| **Stitch Count** | Number of stitches in an embroidery design. Determines pricing tier. | "A left chest, small logo is going to be about 3,000-4,000 stitches... a big logo on the front of a chest, you're probably looking at 8,000-9,000 to 10,000 stitches." |
| **Print Colors** | Number of ink colors in a screen print design. Determines pricing column in matrix. | "It's a one color print" / "If it's a three-color print and your setup is $20 per color..." |
| **Vector** | Vector graphic format required for high-quality screen printing. | "It's all vector. It's exported as vector. Because it has to be super high-quality for the prints." |
| **Re-Vector** | Converting a non-vector file to vector format for print production. | "A creative request... that could be a quick little re-vector, like a technical art request." |
| **Mockup** | A visual representation of the design on the product. | "Link the designs and showcase and mock them up on the product." |

### Pricing & Charges
| Term | Definition | Trevor's Words |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Decorator Matrix** | A pricing grid: rows = quantity ranges, columns = print colors (screen print) or stitch counts (embroidery). Each cell = per-unit cost. | "Decorator matrixes... your quantity ranges and then your print colors from left to right." |
| **Price Breaks** | Quantity tiers where per-unit cost decreases. | "24 to 35 units, it's $3.65 each. For 36 to 49, it's $3.25, so on and so forth." |
| **Fixed Charge** | A flat fee per location/job regardless of quantity (e.g., setup fee). Can have qty breaks. | "A setup fee... if it's a three-color print and your setup is $20 per color, then you'll see a line item that says $60 setup." |
| **Run Charge** | A per-unit charge that varies by quantity (e.g., puff ink surcharge, printed tags). | "A run charge is a charge that... the cost can differ based on the quantity ordered, but applies to the product." |
| **Setup Fee** | Cost to prepare screens/equipment for a print run. Per color, per location. | "Setup is $20 per color per location." |
| **Margin** | Percentage markup from cost to sale price. | "Blank plus decoration plus margin equals sale price." |
| **Sale Price** | What the customer pays per unit. | "It's that simple. And that's contract." |
| **Raw Cost** | Total cost before margin (blank + decoration + charges). | "The raw cost can display, because the user that is inputting that information... that's all they need to see." |

### Dates & Timeline
| Term | Definition | Trevor's Words |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Enhanced Date** / **In-Hands Date** | The date the finished product must be in the client's hands. THE critical deadline. | "You're going to hear that a ton in this industry. It's called the enhanced date. That is the date that the product is needed enhanced." |
| **Project Deadline** | Internal deadline to complete all work so all enhanced dates can be met. | "Project deadline is the day that the project needs to be completed in order to meet... all of the enhanced dates." |
| **Production Time** | Number of business days to produce the order (e.g., 5-7 days, 7-10 days). | "That product might have a five-day production time, and then there might be two days of shipping." |
| **Turnaround Time** | Production time + shipping time. Must fit within enhanced date. | "So that's a seven-day turnaround time. The bot is going to have to look at that and say, can I meet that turnaround time?" |
| **Ship Date** | The date the order actually ships from the decorator/vendor. | "There's always a ship date, right? That's so crucial." |

### Order & Fulfillment
| Term | Definition | Trevor's Words |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Purchase Order (PO)** | Document sent to vendor/decorator authorizing production. | "You have a purchase order related to that order. And that purchase order is going to state to our decorator, I need you to print..." |
| **Split Shipment** / **Multi-Destination Shipment** | An order shipped to multiple addresses with different size/qty breakdowns per destination. | "Split shipments or multi-destination shipments. This is huge in our industry, dude." |
| **Floor Stock** | Inventory held in a warehouse for future pull requests. | "Hold 500 on floor stock. And then when it is stocked, it will tell you down to the row in the warehouse, the pallet number..." |
| **Pull Request** | Client request to release cartons from floor stock for shipment. | "They can make what's called a pull request... pull that carton and ship to a destination." |
| **Breaking Cartons** | Opening a sealed carton to pull individual units (to be avoided). | "We don't want to allow our clients to break cartons. That's a nightmare." |
| **Freight** | Shipping cost added to orders. | "I have to add shipping. I have to go service. I have to add freight right here." |
| **Spoilage** | Defective/damaged units in a completed order. | "How many were spoiled? If there was 4% spoilage rate, then the job is eligible for a reprint." |
| **Reprint** | Re-doing defective units at the decorator's expense (if their fault). | "A reprint ticket or a reprint request needs to go through to the vendor." |

### Business Structure & Roles
| Term | Definition | Trevor's Words |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Outside Sales Rep** | Lead generator / prospector. Brings in new business. | "An outside sales rep, their job is to get interest. Who wants merch?" |
| **Inside Sales Coordinator** / **Account Manager** | Day-to-day client handler. Knows vendors, products, decoration, project management. | "An inside sales coordinator is knowledgeable on vendors, knowledgeable on product, knowledgeable on decoration, project management." |
| **Decorator** / **Print Shop** | The vendor who applies decoration to blank garments. | "I have a printer right now in Chicago... a printer in Texas... a printer in Virginia... a printer in Tennessee." |
| **Supplier** | Company that sells blank products (e.g., S&S Activewear). | "The number one supplier of blank apparel in the world right now is a company called S&S Activewear." |
| **Vendor** | General term for any external business partner (supplier, decorator, or both). | "A vendor is, like, just general. It's a vendor. We all use vendors. But the vendor type..." |
| **Merch Agency** | A business that manages the entire merch lifecycle for clients (not just printing). | "This is merch agency. This is not a t-shirt company." |
| **Distributor** | A company in the promo products distribution channel. | Referenced in context of Boundless Network's distributor model. |

### Client & Project Terminology
| Term | Definition | Trevor's Words |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Defined Inquiry** | Client knows exactly what they want: product, qty, color, logo, date. | "Jeremy would come to me and say, I have an event coming up. I need 100 T-shirts. They need to be black. And here is my logo." |
| **Undefined Inquiry** | Client has assets/interest but needs guidance. | "Jeremy may come to me and say, Trev, I have a logo. I don't know where to start." |
| **Deck** | A visual product presentation/catalog created for a client. | "A full merch deck... the graphics team came out with this. And you can see everything." |
| **Creative Request** | A formal request for design/art services. | "A creative request would be... a quick little re-vector... I need you to build me a full presentation, a beautiful deck." |
| **Client Art** | Artwork/branding/style guide files provided by the client. | "Client Art. Their branding and style guide, that should be high-level print files." |
| **Production Files** | Print-ready files linked to specific decoration locations. | "A production file should link to a decoration location on a product." |
| **Payment Terms** | Net 30, prepay, etc. | "The payment terms, is it net 30, is it prepay." |
| **Order Entry** | The process of entering a confirmed order into the system (currently Salesforce). | "V1 of this app is... just order entry into Boundless' software." |
